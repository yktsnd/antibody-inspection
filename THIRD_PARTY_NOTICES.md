# Third-party materials

- **3Dmol.js 2.5.3**, University of Pittsburgh, BSD-3-Clause. Bundled browser build and license: `media/3Dmol-min.js`, `media/3Dmol-LICENSE.txt`, `media/3Dmol-min.js.LICENSE.txt`. Upstream: https://github.com/3dmol/3Dmol.js .
- **ANARCII 2.0.8**, Oxford Protein Informatics Group, BSD-3-Clause. Installed via PyPI, not embedded in the VSIX. Upstream: https://github.com/oxpig/ANARCII . The installed package contains pretrained numbering resources.
- **Biopython 1.85**: https://biopython.org/wiki/License . **Gemmi 0.7.5**, MPL-2.0: https://github.com/project-gemmi/gemmi . **PyTorch**, BSD-style license: https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch . Dependency licenses remain in the installed runtime.
- **UniProt reviewed human immunoglobulin constant sequences**, CC BY 4.0: https://www.uniprot.org/help/license . The local pack stores accession, entry version, source URL, retrieved sequence and digest for each entry. Rebuild script: `scripts/build-constant-reference.py`. Citation: The UniProt Consortium, UniProt: the Universal Protein Knowledgebase. Exact release-specific provenance is in `references/constant-domains.json`.
- **PDB 1N8Z**, Cho HS et al., Structure of the extracellular region of HER2 alone and in complex with the Herceptin Fab, Nature 421 (2003), DOI: 10.1038/nature01392. https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1N8Z . Coordinates are distributed under the wwPDB data-access policy: https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies . Example sequences come from the deposited polymer entities, including residues absent from the coordinate model.

No private experimental records or synthetic reference cohorts are bundled. Package installation is distinct from permission to redistribute arbitrary third-party model weights; no local structure-prediction weights are included.

- **promb 1.0.2**, MIT, https://github.com/MSDLLCpapers/promb . Installed separately. The human OAS reference contains 9-mers occurring in at least 10% of subjects; exact packaged reference SHA-256 and counts are stored with each result. OASis peptide identity is not an immunogenicity prediction.
- **ImmuneBuilder 1.2**, BSD-3-Clause, Copyright 2022 Brennan Abanades Kenyon, https://github.com/oxpig/ImmuneBuilder . The local adapter uses its neural models, ensemble and refinement code with explicitly identified ANARCII input preparation. Official model files are downloaded separately from Zenodo release https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7258553 and checked against published MD5 hashes; SHA-256 is retained in prediction provenance. No weights are redistributed in this package. OpenMM, PDBFixer and ANARCI are installed dependencies; their license notices remain in the runtime.
- **Published TAP2 numerical reference populations**, Raybould MIJ et al., Communications Biology (2024), https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-023-05744-8 , Supplementary Data 1, CC BY 4.0. `references/tap2-populations.json` is an extracted adaptation of two sheets; workbook digest, sheet, row interval, column, selection and duplicate rules accompany each metric. Attribution and source values are preserved. The licensed SAbBox TAP engine is not included.

- **ANARCI amino-acid germline reference snapshot**, extracted from `anarci.germlines` in package 2026.2.13.2. ANARCI source is BSD-3-Clause; the full notice is retained in `references/ANARCI-LICENSE.txt`. The reference adaptation contains 1,791 V/J entries aligned to IMGT positions; provenance and the source-file digest are stored in `references/germline-aa.json`. It is a fixed historical snapshot, not a complete current IMGT release. IMGT data/metadata terms: https://www.imgt.org/about/termsofuse.php (CC BY 4.0; IMGT tool-use terms are separate). Protein similarity cannot resolve synonymous alleles or assign rearrangement history.
